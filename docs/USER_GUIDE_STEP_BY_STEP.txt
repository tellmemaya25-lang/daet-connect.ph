CONNECT-DAET STEP-BY-STEP USER GUIDE

This guide lists the main features to explore for each role, based on the current app. Text in quotation marks is an app label. Routes are included where useful.

START HERE - ALL USERS

1. Create an account at /signup.
2. Confirm your email, then sign in at /login.
3. Open /settings to review your profile.
4. Open /tours/notifications to see updates.
5. Operators and Guides must apply for their role after signing in. They need approval before using role-specific tools.

TOURIST

1. Find a tour. Visit /tours/browse. Search for trips and try the available place and price filters.
2. Check tour details. Open a trip to see its price, meeting place, directions, itinerary, inclusions, operator information, and reviews.
3. Plan your visit. Open /tours/plan. Enter your request, dates, group size, and starting city when asked. You can also try a sample plan without dates.
4. Explore the plan. Review the stops, estimated times, map, entrance fees, travel information, and tours you can book.
5. Change or save the plan. Try editing, moving, adding, removing, or swapping stops. Choose a pace or travel option, then save the plan or share a view-only link. Offline saving is automatic when available.
6. Ask the assistant. Use /tours/help or /tours/messages/ai to ask about Daet. The assistant can help with planning, but it does not book a tour.
7. Request a booking. On the trip page, select an available date and price option, enter the group size and lead contact details, then send the request.
8. Wait for confirmation. Check /tours/bookings/[id]. The operator must confirm the request before you pay.
9. Pay after confirmation. Use the payment option shown. If manual payment is available, follow the instructions and upload proof.
10. Open your ticket. Go to /tours/bookings/[id]/ticket. The ticket QR appears after the booking is fully paid. You can print it; it may also be saved automatically for offline use.
11. Manage changes. Check your booking for cancellation updates, operator call-offs, and any new-date or refund choices.
12. During and after the tour. Use messages and optional location sharing. After the booking is marked completed, submit a review and check your rewards.
13. Explore other public pages: Attractions, Events, Souvenirs, FAQ, and Help.

TOUR OPERATOR

1. Apply. Submit the business application at /tours/operator/register. Check /tours/operator/pending while waiting for approval.
2. Check the dashboard. Visit /tours/operator/dashboard to see bookings waiting for you, revenue, occupancy, ratings, tours by date, today's tours, and revenue by package.
3. Create a tour package. Open /tours/operator/packages/create. Complete the sections for details, inclusions, pricing, itinerary, and images. Then submit it for review.
4. Edit your package. Use /tours/operator/packages/[id]/edit to update details, prices, stops, or images. The available save and submit actions depend on the package status.
5. Set tour dates and capacity. Open the package availability page to add dates and seats.
6. Manage bookings. At /tours/operator/bookings, confirm or decline requests and follow their status. You can mark a tour completed on or after its date.
7. Assign a Guide. Choose an approved, available Guide for a booking. Review and respond to schedule-swap requests.
8. Manage payments. Review manual payment proofs and update payment records. Record a refund after it has actually been sent.
9. Manage cancellations and call-offs. Review cancellation requests. If you call off a tour, enter a reason and follow the available next steps.
10. Check revenue and payment settings. Use /tours/operator/payments for the revenue report and CSV download. Use /tours/operator/settings to review business and payment details.
11. Use live-tour tools. Open /tours/operator/live to check live-tour status when available.
12. Manage reviews and merchandise. Reply to reviews. At /tours/operator/merch, create or edit products and check their review status.
13. Use messages and notifications. Contact tourists, Guides, or the Tourism Office and check for updates.

TOUR GUIDE

1. Apply. Submit the application at /tours/guide/register. Check /tours/guide/pending while waiting for approval.
2. Check your dashboard. Visit /tours/guide/dashboard to see today's and upcoming tours and your availability calendar.
3. Set your availability. Update the calendar and save your changes. Some dates may be unavailable if you have an active booking.
4. Check your tours. Open /tours/guide/my-tours to see assigned tours, guest-list links, ticket-scanning links, and completed tours. You can also request a schedule swap.
5. Open the guest roster. Review the booking group size and special requests. Guest names may not be available; when they are missing, check in the group as a whole.
6. Check in guests. Use /tours/guide/check-in to scan a ticket QR or use the camera/paste-link option. Check-in is limited to the right Guide and a confirmed, fully paid booking on the tour date.
7. Show your Guide badge. Open /tours/guide/my-qr ("My QR Code"). The public verification page is /tours/verify/[token]. This badge QR is not a tourist's ticket QR.
8. Use live-tour tools. Start a live tour, allow location access, review the map and stops, then end the tour when finished.
9. Check messages and updates. Watch for assignment, swap, or call-off messages and notifications.

ADMIN / TOURISM OFFICE

1. Check the dashboard. Open /tours/admin to see review queues, management links, and recent activity.
2. Review Operator applications. Use /tours/admin/operators to check applications and authorized documents, then approve or reject.
3. Review Guide applications. Use /tours/admin/guides to check Guide profiles and protected identity documents, then approve or reject.
4. Review tour packages. Open /tours/admin/packages to see Pending Approval, Published, and Archived sections. Open a package to review its status and notes.
5. Review merchandise. Use /tours/admin/merch to check products waiting for approval, then approve or reject them with a note.
6. Manage attractions and local information. Use /tours/admin/attractions for attraction listings and map pins, /tours/admin/fares for fares, and /tours/admin/guide-places for local places.
7. Manage events. Use /tours/admin/events to add or edit events and check their status. The public events calendar is at /tours/events.
8. Manage emergency numbers. Use /tours/admin/emergency to add a number, mark it "Checked today," or delete it.
9. Moderate reviews. At /tours/admin/reviews, filter reviews, hide a review with a reason, or show a hidden review again.
10. Review assistant feedback. At /tours/admin/feedback, check "Not helpful," "Helpful," or "All," and review the questions, answers, answer type, date, and page.
11. Check analytics. Use /tours/admin/analytics to choose a date range and review bookings, revenue, check-ins, ratings, and other available totals.
12. Check messages and activity. Review messages, notifications, and audit activity when needed.

IMPORTANT THINGS TO KNOW

- The current Admin package screen does not offer a Rejected status. Do not show package rejection as an available Admin action.
- Tourist bookings collect a group size and lead contact details, not each guest's name or age.
- The tourist merchandise page is a showcase/contact page, not an in-app checkout.
- The emergency-number page has no edit action.
- Offline saving is automatic when available; it is not a setting the user switches on or off.
- Use test or redacted data for IDs, tickets, receipts, contact details, and locations.
