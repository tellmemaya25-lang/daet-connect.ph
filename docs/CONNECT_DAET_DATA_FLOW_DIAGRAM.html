Context DFD — Level 0

One process represents the entire Daet-Connect System. Database details are hidden at this level; only external actors and services are shown.

Detailed DFD — Level 1

The main process IDs remain consistent across the focused views: 1.0 users, 2.0 bookings, 3.0 itinerary, 4.0 check-in, 5.0 payments, and 6.0 packages. Support processes 7.0–11.0 cover content, messaging, rewards, the travel assistant, guide operations, and live safety.

1.0 — Manage Users & Accreditation

Tourist, operator, and guide account/application data is checked against the session and role; the Tourism Office handles protected review actions.

6.0 — Manage Packages & Availability

Operators maintain packages and open dates; the Tourism Office approves or rejects submissions. Published records feed browsing and booking.

2.0 & 5.0 — Bookings, Payments & PayMongo

Package, price, date, guest count, and capacity are validated before a booking is created. Payment status is recorded separately from the booking.

3.0 — Generate AI Itinerary

The rule-based planner builds the schedule from Daet data. Gemini can interpret or explain a request, but is optional; it does not confirm bookings.

4.0 — Verify QR Check-in

The guide scans a signed QR ticket or uses the roster. The server checks authorization, assignment, tour date, booking status, and full payment before recording the check-in.

7.0–11.0 — Content, Communication, Engagement & Safety

These aligned support rows summarize the whole-system modules outside the core booking journey.

Whole-system flows & safeguards

The focused diagrams show the main DFD paths. These notes tie them back to the complete role workflows and shared services in CONNECT-DAET.

Tour Operator workflowApply for accreditation; draft and maintain packages; set availability and capacity; confirm or decline bookings; assign approved, available guides; review manual receipts; call off or complete tours. Public merchandise is a showcase/contact flow, not an in-app checkout.
Tour Guide workflowApply and submit private documents; set availability; receive assignments; request schedule swaps; use the QR scanner or roster for check-in; opt into live-tour location sharing and send a separated flare when needed. Check-in rejects invalid or unauthorized requests without writing a record.
Tourism Office workflowReview operator and guide applications, packages and merchandise; maintain attractions, events, fares and emergency contacts; moderate reviews; review assistant feedback; access analytics and audit records.
Assistant & plannerThe itinerary engine and data-backed travel content work without Gemini. The assistant is read-only for public data and permitted booking data; it links to booking pages but does not book, confirm payment, promise refunds, or invent schedules.
Shared platformNext.js pages, Server Actions and API routes run on Vercel. Supabase Auth, Postgres RLS/RPCs, private Storage and Realtime protect account, booking, chat and file data.
External servicesPayMongo checkout events are signature-verified and rechecked before ledger updates. Gemini and Google Maps/Routes are optional; basic answers and estimated route fallbacks remain available.
Booking integrityPrices and open seats are read from the database; capacity is checked transactionally before a booking reserves space. A payment event does not itself mean a booking is confirmed.
Check-in integrityThe server validates the signed ticket, tour date, booking/payment state, assigned guide and caller role. Duplicate scans are harmless and idempotent.
Private filesAccreditation documents and manual payment receipt proofs live in protected Supabase Storage, not public tables or browser code.
Live locationTourist sharing is opt-in; guide location is limited to an active live-tour window. Trip mode shares town-level location, while tour mode shares the live position. Only the latest position is kept, and ending or expiring the session removes live-position and flare records.
